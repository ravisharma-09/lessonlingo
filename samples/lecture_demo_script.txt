Welcome to today's science lesson. Today we will learn about the water cycle.
Water moves between the land, oceans, and the atmosphere.
First, heat from the sun turns liquid water into vapour. This is called evaporation.
Next, the vapour rises, cools down, and forms clouds. This is called condensation.
Then the clouds release water as rain or snow. This is called precipitation.
Plants also play a part. They take in water through their roots and release it through their leaves. This is called transpiration.
The water cycle keeps moving, and it gives us the fresh water that we drink and use every day.
Please remember these four words: evaporation, condensation, precipitation, and transpiration.
